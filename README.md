# CampusSpace

Abstract

Venue booking on college campuses is typically handled through spreadsheets, manual approvals, or disconnected systems, leading to double bookings, delayed approvals, and inefficient use of available spaces. While some institutions have adopted basic digital booking portals, these systems largely function as static calendars — they show whether a room is free or taken, but do not help organizers determine whether a venue is actually the right fit for their event, nor do they detect when a "booked" space goes unused. CampusSpace addresses this gap by shifting the focus from simple scheduling to intelligent venue matching and utilization tracking. Given an event's type, expected attendance, required facilities, and preferred timing, the system recommends the most suitable available venues rather than merely listing open slots, using historical booking patterns to rank options by fit. A QR-based check-in mechanism detects no-shows and automatically releases unused bookings back into the pool, reducing artificial scarcity caused by unclaimed reservations. The platform further anticipates high-demand periods based on past booking trends, allowing organizers to plan ahead rather than react to conflicts after they occur. For administrators, CampusSpace offers a utilization dashboard that reveals underused or overbooked facilities, supporting data-driven decisions about campus resource planning, while low-risk requests are auto-approved to reduce manual workload for faculty coordinators. By combining smart recommendation, real-time availability, and utilization analytics into a single centralized platform, CampusSpace aims to make campus event planning more efficient, transparent, and responsive than existing booking systems that address only the scheduling layer of the problem.
